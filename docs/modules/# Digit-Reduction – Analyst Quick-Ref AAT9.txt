## 5. Common Misreads

1. **“Orig” row already ≤ 3 digits**  
   → It’s NOT a reduction artefact; it’s a small R2 box. Treat carefully.
2. **Caught by length ceiling** (Method T)  
   → The survivor may still hold eliminated mirrors; cross-check Method C to verify.
3. **Long-String 2 Nulls**  
   → If Set1 Draw-6 col1 is blank, the Evening side of LS-2 collapses; ignore cross-grid symmetry for that set.

## 6. FAQ Snippets

*“Why do some Method D rows end in ‘0’ only?”*  
Single-mirror rule ate the lone mirror and left zeros which were not mirrors of any draw digit — harmless filler.

*“Midday vs Evening – which wins?”*  
Over the last year, Evening survivor hits **1.3×** more often but pays marginally less (lower odd-spread). Use combo tickets.
