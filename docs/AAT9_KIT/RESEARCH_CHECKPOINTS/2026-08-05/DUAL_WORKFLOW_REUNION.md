# Dual Workflow Reunion

The Template Workflow and Extraction Zone Workflow are complementary, not
competing replacements.

## Master Deep Review

Question: What did the current programmed system detect, preserve, aggregate,
translate, rank, emit, withhold, or lose?

Its durable roles are source and timing verification, tool inventory, evidence
lineage, identity-level tracking, route and width auditing, first-loss
diagnosis, regression comparison, and preserve/fix/test/defer decisions.

## Extraction Zone Review

Question: What should a pathway-first analyst or future extraction engine have
seen directly in the three-variant string environment?

Its durable roles are independent candidate-family discovery, Zone 1 feeder
analysis, Zone 2 progression analysis, typed Zone 3 events, reduction receipts,
candidate merit, competitor burden, box/order routes, and opportunity or
abstention classification.

The blind extraction pass is frozen before result reveal. Winner-aware review
discovers mechanisms but receives no predictive credit by itself.

## Reunion

For matched outcomes, freeze both lanes independently and then compare exact,
canonical-box, ordered-VTRAC, boxed-VTRAC, pair, route, width, and state-class
receipts. The reconciliation identifies whether the first material loss was:

- missing raw detection;
- tool export loss;
- Arena synthesis or promotion loss;
- translation loss;
- candidate compression or ordering loss;
- emission or funding loss;
- a hindsight-only hypothesis that fails blind testing.

Repeated differences become bounded shadow-harness candidates. Only mechanisms
that improve matched-width results and survive negative controls, untouched
Gold Days, and live shadow testing may enter production behavior.

The durable principle is:

> Master Deep Review establishes what the current system did. Extraction Zone
> Review establishes what a pathway-first system should have seen. Harness
> testing determines which difference deserves to become code.
