## Purpose And Separation

This is the dedicated state-by-state evidence log for Core Extraction Zone
example reviews. It records the complete predictive analysis, result-aware
reverse-engineering analysis, and Gold Day synthesis for each reviewed case.

The responsibilities remain separated:

- `CORE_EXTRACTION_ZONE_AND_CONVEY_TRAINING_LOG.md` is the training,
  terminology, doctrine, and analyst-procedure authority.
- This file is the chronological execution and example-analysis record.
- The Deep Example Review Journal keeps durable macro findings, architecture
  implications, and development decisions discovered across examples.
- Template and harness artifacts remain separate evidence products and may
  consume findings from this log only at their appropriate later stages.

Do not place new training doctrine here merely because it is discovered
during an example. Record the observation in the example first. Promote it to
the training guide or Deep Example Review Journal only after its evidence
status and generality are clear.

## Gold Day Review Unit

The review unit is one complete Gold Day. Each supplied dataset snapshot is
analyzed once as a shared pre-result environment, then evaluated against both
Midday and Evening results for every applicable state.

Each Gold Day must record:

- Gold Day date and state inventory;
- exact artifact paths, creation timestamps, and content hashes where
  available;
- latest included draw and cutoff for Midday, Evening, and Combined;
- whether result values were hidden when each analytical section was frozen;
- extraction-zone coordinate availability and any rendering limitation;
- AUX CORE and other supporting-artifact availability;
- result source and reveal time;
- missing, stale, contradictory, or regenerated artifacts.

## Single-Snapshot Timing Boundary

The current workflow supplies one pre-day snapshot and uses it to evaluate
both periods:

- Midday is the fresher forecast horizon because no same-day result has yet
  occurred after the snapshot.
- Evening is evaluated from the same frozen snapshot and therefore does not
  include that day's Midday result. It is a valid pre-result analysis but a
  deliberately older information horizon.
- No Midday result, winner artifact, or result-derived candidate may be used
  to revise the frozen Evening analysis in this mode.
- Declared recent-draw reduction uses `Midday + Combined` for the Midday
  target and `Evening + Midday` for the Evening target. In the pre-day
  snapshot, Combined duplicates the latest Evening draw and must not be
  counted as a second Evening reduction lineage.
- Midday and Evening performance must be reported separately before any
  full-day aggregation. A possible Midday advantage is a timing hypothesis,
  not an assumed result.
- Evening remains analytically important for persistence, carry, decay,
  cross-period convey, and testing whether a strong pre-day environment can
  remain useful through the full day.

Use these clock labels:

- `PRE_DAY_SNAPSHOT__MIDDAY_TARGET`
- `PRE_DAY_SNAPSHOT__EVENING_TARGET__NO_MIDDAY_REFRESH`

When same-day refresh becomes available, preserve the pre-day report and add
a separately versioned `POST_MIDDAY_REFRESH__EVENING_TARGET` report. Never
overwrite or silently replace the original Evening analysis. The two clocks
must be compared as distinct forecast-horizon cohorts.

## Required Analysis Sequence

### Phase P0 - Shared Pre-Result Environment

With all target results hidden:

- inventory all Midday, Evening, and Combined raw strings independently;
- map and analyze locked Extraction Zones 1, 2, and 3;
- register candidate identities, coordinates, maturity, progression,
  competitors, and contradictions;
- run only declared Digit Reduction routes after the raw inventory;
- freeze each variant before joining literal, canonical, ordered-VTRAC,
  boxed-VTRAC, pair, double, or other typed relationships;
- join AUX CORE only after the string evidence and candidate inventory are
  frozen.

This shared analysis may be referenced by both target periods without being
counted twice as independent evidence.

### Phase P1 - Period-Specific Predictive Analysis

For Midday and Evening separately:

- identify the strongest eligible candidate pathways and their rank depth;
- preserve separate family-merit and order-merit vectors;
- state whether each pathway is carry, trigger, currentness, contradiction,
  or abstention evidence;
- record every equal-or-stronger competitor;
- emit only merit-supported boxed, canonical, ordered-lane, straight,
  double, pair, or nearby-key routes;
- enumerate every route member and cumulative width;
- freeze the target-period conclusion before any result is revealed.

Allowed conclusions include `HIGH_BOX_OPPORTUNITY`,
`HIGH_STRAIGHT_OPPORTUNITY`, `VTRAC_TERRITORY_ONLY`,
`CONFLICTED_OR_WATCH`, `NO_CREDIBLE_PATHWAY`, and abstention.

### Phase R1 - Result-Aware Deep Reverse Engineering

The reveal analysis is not merely a scorecard for Phase P1. It has two
equally required jobs:

1. Grade what the frozen predictive analysis recognized, ranked, translated,
   filtered, and retained.
2. Conduct a fresh, comprehensive winner-origin and convey autopsy across all
   raw strings, zones, progression paths, declared reductions, variants,
   VTRAC relationships, AUX CORE evidence, and downstream preservation.

For each Midday and Evening result, record:

- exact literal, canonical box, ordered-VTRAC, boxed-VTRAC, pair, double,
  hidden-core, extended-cluster, and decay relationships separately;
- every valid winner or winner-family coordinate, including structures the
  renderer did not highlight;
- Zone 1 origin, Zone 2 progression, Zone 3 survivor/repeat behavior, and
  cross-zone overlap with lineage de-duplication;
- vertical R-row stability, horizontal persistence, Set/Draw movement,
  contraction, survival, re-entry, concentration, currentness, and
  competitor behavior;
- whether reduction revealed, confirmed, compressed, destroyed, or
  over-reduced the target pathway;
- cross-variant literal, canonical, ordered-VTRAC, boxed-VTRAC, pair, and
  contradiction relationships;
- nearby structures that supplied legitimate order or permutation clues;
- AUX CORE alignment, conflict, companion, due, positional, or restraint
  roles without retroactively making AUX the string-pattern source;
- the smallest defensible family and order products, their exact members,
  and their widths;
- first material success or loss stage: recognition, progression, ranking,
  reduction, AUX resolution, synthesis, translation, filtering, ordering, or
  final selection;
- what should be preserved, changed, tested, or left unresolved.

Any valid pathway found only after reveal is valuable reverse-engineering
evidence and must be recorded as `POST_RESULT_DISCOVERY`. It receives no
predictive credit unless later converted into a frozen pre-result rule and
validated on complete unseen cohorts.

### Phase R2 - Full-Day And Gold Day Synthesis

Only after both periods and all states are complete:

- compare Midday and Evening without hiding their different snapshot ages;
- identify repeated hit origins, maturity states, extraction pathways,
  conversion losses, and abstention conditions;
- measure target rank, containment, exact candidate burden, misses, and
  decay by identity and route type;
- distinguish state diversification from bounded pathway persistence;
- inventory improvements for later template analysis and shadow harnessing;
- avoid promoting one selected success into a universal rule.
