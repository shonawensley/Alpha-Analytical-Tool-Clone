3. The most important independent finding: base-rate normalization
Why 26/28 sounds more powerful than it is

The raw phrase:

Winning boxed-VTRAC territory was in the zones in 26/28 outcomes

sounds extraordinary.

But the corrected target-plus-Combined Zone 1/2 candidate inventory contains, on average:

approximately 192 unique literal triples
approximately 64% of all 1,000 outcomes through canonical families
approximately 66% through ordered VTRAC codes
approximately 98.5% through boxed VTRAC territories

That means a random result would almost always share some boxed-VTRAC territory with something in these zones.

So:

Boxed-VTRAC presence is a territory inventory, not yet a selective prediction.

This does not make VTRAC useless. It clarifies its role:

VTRAC presence:
broad neighborhood availability

VTRAC ranking:
which neighborhood deserves attention

Canonical translation:
which digits inside the neighborhood matter

Order resolution:
which permutations deserve straight consideration

Route formation:
how many actual lines must be retained

The V2 objective should not be:

Can we find the winner’s VTRAC somewhere?

It should be:

Can we rank the winning VTRAC above the other territories,
shrink it toward the correct canonical family,
resolve order where evidence permits,
and do so at useful width?
