## Purpose

The deep example-review process exists to answer one core question:

What is the system already doing correctly, where does the evidence appear, where does it get promoted or lost, and what should we preserve or rebuild when we eventually improve final combination forming, budgeting, metrics, and performance tracking?

This journal should capture:

- Important findings that should not be buried in chat.
- Review methods that make the artifact stack easier to use.
- Evidence-layer definitions that prevent confusion between old infrastructure and Arena-native diagnostics.
- High-value examples where winners reached meaningful finalist zones.
- Questions that need more artifact tracing before they can become conclusions.
- Follow-up work that should feed future translator, budget, and performance-infrastructure development.

## How To Use This Journal

- Separate source facts from interpretation. Do not let a useful theory become a stated fact unless it is supported by artifacts.

## Working Review Questions

These are the main questions to keep asking while reviewing a gold day, state, or hit:

- What did the system see before the draw?
- What did the system actually convert after the draw?
- Was the winner captured in a broad universe, small pack, Play Card row, Arena seed, Brain 1 lane, Brain 2 posture, tracker, or signal stack?
- Was the event an exact hit, canonical box hit, VTRAC-only territory hit, context-only alignment, or no conversion?
- Was the evidence captured and used, captured but underused, captured but not promoted, wrong-lane, decay validated, or absent?
- Which signals survive denominator pressure and false-positive checks?
- Which signal stacks or convergences look sharper than individual signals?
- Which findings are promising but not yet live-playable infrastructure?
- Which findings deserve replay across more days or windows before becoming rules?

## Current Mental Model

As of 2026-07-25, the system should be understood as Arena-centered but not yet
fully Arena-native at the semantic-synthesis, final-combination, and budgeting
layers.

The important layers are:

- Brain 1 / Aggregated Analysis Arena v0: per-state evidence preservation plus
  bounded review synthesis. It summarizes selected evidence into canonical,
  VTRAC-index, family, context-reinforced, survivor, consensus, and watchlist
  surfaces. It is not yet a calibrated semantic candidate-neighborhood engine.
- Brain 2 / Board / Shadow DPL: cross-state relationship and decision-policy
  context. Historical static board order is not a valid analytical ranking and
  must not receive rank credit. The relationship, spillover, tracker, and posture
  evidence remains useful.
- Translation Sandbox: the closest current near-final diagnostic comparison
  zone. It joins Arena/Brain 2/Shadow DPL evidence with Candidate Universe and
  Play Card control-arm evidence, then emits provisional boxed, straight, and
  VTRAC seeds. It is not a pure Arena finalist list and is not a live translator.
- Realized Control Arm: Candidate Universe plus Play Card. Candidate Universe
  independently reads raw/sharepack and per-tool Arena-era surfaces; Play Card
  reads Candidate Universe. This branch runs in the same cadence but is not a
  serial `Aggregated Arena -> Candidate Universe -> Play Card` pipeline.
- Experimental Deep Review Routes: Structural Convergence Anchor, Bounded VTRAC
  Closure, and Merit-Allocated VTRAC Cluster slates. These are separate,
  winner-blind shadow candidate sources for comparison and harness learning.
  They are not current-system, selected, funded, or realized outputs.
- Post-run Quantification Stack: the promotion filter. It tells us whether observed winner alignment is sharp enough, too broad, noisy, underused, wrong-lane, stack-supported, or hypothesis-worthy.

The practical implication:

- Arena evidence can be strong even when the old Play Card did not convert optimally.
- A Play Card hit is meaningful as control-arm realization, but it does not prove
  that Aggregated Arena selected, ranked, or translated that candidate.
- A sandbox or attribution hit can be very valuable diagnostically, but it should not be counted as live-playable final prediction infrastructure unless it reached a realized output surface.
- A feature being designed, present, or preserved does not prove that the current
  synthesis consumes it, calibrates it, or applies its intended interactions.
- Missing semantic synthesis limits how much tool intelligence influences
  candidate promotion; it does not erase the underlying tool evidence.

## Finalized-Zone Definition

There is not one single clean final-combination layer yet. That is one reason
this review process matters. The "finalized-esque" zone below is a comparison
region across parallel evidence surfaces, not one serial pipeline or one final
prediction list.

For now, the "finalized-esque" zone is the overlap among:

- Brain 1 aggregated Arena synthesis.
- Brain 2 / scoreboard / shadow DPL state posture.
- Translation Sandbox seeds.
- Candidate Universe packs.
- Play Card strategy rows.
- Stage 2B stack findings.
- Translator Rule Hypothesis Queue candidates.

Use this strength scale when describing evidence:

- Strong near-final evidence: small Candidate Universe packs, roughly 6 to 10 candidates; Play Card exact or boxed rows in roughly 12 to 24 candidates; positive conversion regression rows; Stage 2B or hypothesis-queue rows marked for bounded testing.
- Moderate near-final evidence: Play Card rows around 36 candidates; Candidate Universe packs around 36 candidates; sandbox boxed or VTRAC seeds; Arena finalist signatures that point to the right territory but require narrowing.
- Weak evidence alone: full Candidate Universe union around 171 to 233 candidates; broad VTRAC-only territory; scoreboard rank alone; broad context or tracker support without bounded candidate evidence.

This scale is a working interpretation rule, not a permanent law. It should be updated as more examples are reviewed.

## Guardrails

- Historical static scoreboard order is not an analytical rank and receives no
  predictive credit. A future validated ranking may measure state opportunity,
  but it still would not be an exact-number prediction list.
- VTRAC-only evidence is territory evidence, not exact or canonical-box evidence.
- Winner-side alignment alone overfits. Always ask how often the same signal fired elsewhere and missed.
- Denominator pressure matters. A signal that appears on winners but also appears everywhere is not promotable by itself.
- Do not blend control-arm hits with Arena diagnostics. Label the source surface clearly.
- Do not treat bonus/fireball sidecar behavior as standard draw conversion unless the prediction source explicitly used that sidecar.
- The custom hit report is a synthesis layer. The quantification stack is the promotion-readiness layer.
- Do not create durable predictive rules from one day or one window alone. Use cross-window confirmation before promotion.
- When in doubt, preserve the finding as a hypothesis instead of forcing it into the system.

## 2026-07-28 - Entry 024 - Positional Width And VTRAC Two-Axis Interpretation

Context:

- The Positional harness reported width-16 exact coverage of `1.51%`,
  canonical-box coverage of `6.11%`, and boxed-VTRAC coverage of `34.01%`.
- Those figures can be misunderstood as grading all available VTRAC
  combinations or the system's complete extraction ability. They grade only
  one bounded native Positional shortlist.
- The intended system philosophy places substantial value on first trapping a
  winner's boxed VTRAC territory, then using string patterns, boxed-combination
  evidence, ordered VTRAC lanes, Positional evidence, and other support to
  narrow that territory.

Finding:

The width-16 figures have a narrow and exact meaning.

For each of `1,129` frozen pre-result events, the native Positional tool emitted
an ordered state-level shortlist of up to 16 Pick-3 literals. The post-result
grader then asked three different questions:

1. `Exact@16`: did the literal winner itself appear in the first 16 rows?
   This occurred in `17 / 1,129` events, or `1.51%`.
2. `Canonical@16`: did any row have the same three digits as the winner,
   ignoring order? This occurred in `69 / 1,129` events, or `6.11%`.
3. `VTRAC@16`: did any row belong to the same boxed VTRAC index as the winner?
   This occurred in `384 / 1,129` events, or `34.01%`.

The native shortlist is built from aggregated positional digits, Cartesian
seeds, repeat-endcap seeds, and lane-concordance seeds, then ranked and capped
at 16 rows. Candidate VTRAC identity can be retained or optionally boosted, but
the builder does not:

- expand a selected boxed VTRAC index into its full reference corridor;
- emit every canonical box belonging to that index;
- emit every ordered VSTRAIGHTS lane belonging to that index;
- emit every permutation of each related canonical;
- evaluate all string-table extraction pathways;
- represent the total performance of Arena, AUX CORE, Play Card, or the full
  system.

Therefore, `1.51%` and `6.11%` do not disprove the boxed-VTRAC-first extraction
philosophy. They show that the current Positional shortlist alone rarely
completed the final literal or canonical narrowing. The `34.01%` result is the
more relevant Positional receipt for territory trapping, but it still requires
candidate-burden and false-positive context.

### Correct VTRAC relationship model

The comprehensive VTRAC reference is a valid and important identity and
expansion resource. Its layers must remain distinct:

1. `Boxed VTRAC index`: the broad mirror-related territory.
2. `Canonical box member`: one exact digit set inside that territory,
   independent of order.
3. `Ordered VSTRAIGHTS lane`: one positional VTRAC-code lane inside the same
   territory, independent of the exact mirror choice at each position.
4. `Exact literal`: the final ordered Pick-3 value.

Canonical boxes and ordered VSTRAIGHTS lanes are best understood as two
different narrowing axes inside a boxed index, not as interchangeable labels.
Their intersection can isolate an exact literal.

For Connecticut winner `091`:

- exact literal: `091`;
- canonical box: `019`;
- ordered VSTRAIGHTS lane: `v152`;
- boxed VTRAC index: `9`.

The canonical permutations of `019` and the eight literals in ordered lane
`v152` intersect at `091`. Boxed index `9` is much broader: it contains six
ordered VSTRAIGHTS lanes and a 48-literal straight closure. This illustrates
the intended extraction opportunity:

```text
string / tool evidence identifies a strong boxed index
-> boxed-table evidence prioritizes canonical members
-> ordered-lane and Positional evidence prioritizes VSTRAIGHTS lanes
-> canonical-member and ordered-lane intersection isolates literals
-> final filtering applies evidence, width, timing, and cost constraints
```

This model connects directly with the user's string-table analysis:

- String patterns can identify lingering or converging VTRAC territories.
- Boxed VTRAC tables can expose the most merit-supported canonical members
  through badges, due context, and cross-variant support.
- VTRAC straight tables preserve ordered mirror-permutation relationships.
- Positional evidence can reinforce position, front/back/end-cap pairs,
  ordered lanes, mirrors, doubles, and permutation choices.
- AUX CORE can expose whether these sources converge on the same index,
  canonical member, lane, or literal without prematurely adding incompatible
  raw scores.

Interpretation:

- The low exact and canonical width-16 rates primarily diagnose an unfinished
  narrowing problem inside one Positional candidate source.
- The higher boxed-VTRAC rate suggests useful territory recall, but `34.01%`
  cannot by itself be called high precision. A 16-literal slate can cover
  several boxed indices, and boxed indices have different corridor sizes.
- Future evaluation must report how many unique boxed indices, ordered lanes,
  canonical boxes, and exact literals were carried. This is the true burden of
  trapping the winner.
- A full VTRAC corridor should count as one selected territory with an explicit
  expansion burden. Its 48 literals must not become 48 independent evidence
  votes.
- A VSTRAIGHTS lane should count as one ordered-lane hypothesis with its
  eight-literal burden. Its members must not become eight independent sources.
- A canonical box should count as one digit-set hypothesis, with its applicable
  permutation burden.
- The VTRAC reference supplies complete relationship truth, not predictive
  merit by itself. Merit must come from pre-result string, Aux, Positional,
