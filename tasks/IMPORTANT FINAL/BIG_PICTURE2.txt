## A. Core Intent
- Use long‑string **hot‑zone boxes** (LS1 7/6/5; LS2 3/1) to reduce down toward **clean 3‑value patterns**.  
- Explore 3‑value patterns even before full reduction—sometimes early steps or raw strings already contain usable signals.
- Scoring should **follow results**, not hope. Favor candidates that persist and repeat across boxes/sets.

## B. Cross‑Chart Reasoning
- Don’t just “find hot zones”; evaluate **pattern positions** inside boxes and **repeats across** Midday/Evening/Combined.  
- Check whether a pattern **moves into super‑hot zones** and whether it **repeats across sets** (Set3→Set2→Set1).  
- Use **own vs. combined** deltas to learn when a candidate strengthens or weakens.

## C. Intelligent Reduction
- Avoid blind elimination of all prior digits + mirrors. Prefer **goal‑aware elimination** that preserves a clean 3‑value set.  
- Watch for **digit shifts / clusters** that reinforce a stable pattern rather than destroy it.

## D. Pattern Repeats & Rates

## 1) Profitability Framing (Actionable)
- **Two operating modes**  
  1) *Investment/ROI mode* (box/straight targets): play within a fixed combination budget such that an expected straight (≈900:1) or box return yields target weekly ROI.  
  2) *Progressive‑wager mode* (responsible, capped): up to N draws (e.g., 6) with a tight stop‑loss; full‑cycle losses must be outnumbered by within‑cycle wins.
- **Entry rules (gates):** minimum score S*, synergy across sections, and at least one *premium* indicator (e.g., 3‑value repeat + cross‑chart match).  
  If gates aren’t met → **no play**.
- **Play selection heuristic:** Favor candidates that: (a) survive to a clean **3‑value set**; (b) **repeat across boxes** or **sets**; (c) show **VTRAC alignment**; (d) land in **super‑hot** zones or **tail consensus**.
- **State selection:** We have many states; allocate bankroll only where thresholds trigger. **No rush—profits compound when selectivity is high.**

## 2) Canonical Signals (What the system should score)
### 2.1 Long‑String (LS1 7/6/5; LS2 3/1)
- **3‑value lingering pattern** appears at any step or near tail.
- **Horizontal persistence**: pattern progresses 7→…→1 without disappearing, especially into **col‑3/2/1** hot zones.
- **Vertical coverage**: same canonical (boxed) pattern shows in R2/R4/R6/R8.
- **Cross‑set persistence**: Set3→Set2→Set1 carryover in the same column.
- **Own ↔ Combined agreement**: a candidate that exists in *own* and *combined* modes scores higher.
- **Step location**: appearing early is good; **survives deep** (≤ col‑3) is better; **at tail 1/2/3** is premium.

### 2.2 R2/R4/R6/R8 box stability & permutation hints
- **In‑box stability**: digits survive across R2/R4/R6/R8 after elimination (e.g., same mirror classes remain).
- **Permutation cues**: when order repeats in more than one row/column or across sets, boost *straight* likelihood.
- **Mirror logic**: use mirror pairs as classes; prefer patterns that *keep* crucial classes rather than blindly removing them.

### 2.3 V‑TRAC relationships
- **V‑TRAC index repeat/soon‑due**: repeating or due V‑TRAC groups get a premium.  
- **Cross‑draw VTRAC agreement** (Midday/Evening/Combined) and **cross‑box VTRAC alignment** amplify score.
- **Boxed eight‑combo window**: when a candidate compresses to ≤ 8 V‑TRAC combos, cost to cover is low → profit favorability ↑.

### 2.4 Aux / Control‑Center overlays (compound‑only)
- **Due doubles / mirror‑doubles**, **due pairs**, **due sums**, **key digit floaters**, **Blackapple status**.  
  These operate as **bonus features**; they never override string analysis. Influence should be learned by results.

- `aux.due_double`, `aux.due_pair`, `aux.due_sum`, `aux.blackapple_alert`

- Base score = sum(weights × features).  
- **Synergy bonus** when ≥2 premium features co‑occur (e.g., `ls.is_3value_now` + `vtrac.index_repeat` + `own_combined_agree`).
