2.2 What EB/ES are
From the definition in your validation log: (user)


EB (Exact Boxed) – digits of candidate triad match winner’s digits (order ignored).


ES (Exact Straight) – EB + at least one evidence row with has_straight == 1.


So:


Every winner should have EB=true for its own canonical triad.


Many non‑winners can still have literal_hits>0, because they were literal draws in Set1 rows, but EB is only defined relative to that day’s winners.


2.3 Relationship between the two
So:


“Literal hits” ≠ “this is the winning number”


“Literal hits” = “this triad has explicit Set1 draw support; it might be today’s winner or just a strong historical lane.”


EB/ES are then computed on top of that, by comparing each top candidate’s triad to that date’s winner digits.
