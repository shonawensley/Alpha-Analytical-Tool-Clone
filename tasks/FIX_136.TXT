3) What to double‑touch (with likely root causes & minimal fixes)
A) Winner‑specific spotlight rows are empty

Symptoms: Family evidence (scores, doubles, bonuses) is present; but when we expect a row in “winner spotlight raw” keyed to the winner’s canonical, it’s missing, and the join yields NaNs on row_score, row_type, row_why, etc.

Most likely causes & targeted patches

Canonical mismatch (winner ↔ row)

Why it happens: winner strings like 680 vs. canonical like 068 (leading zero) or whitespace/padding differences; or our enricher’s _canonical does not exactly match Stable’s canon.

Patch: Replace the local canonicalizer with Stable’s canonical helper (SSOT); normalize winners via the same canon() used to write scores.csv. (This is exactly what FIX_126 requested.)

Spotlight filter is too strict (exact‑type only)

Why it happens: The “winner spotlight” filter may be requiring a specific type (e.g., straight) or specific section when the winner actually appears in a different row‑type/section in the underlying evidence; result: spotlight totals exist at the family level, but nothing qualifies as a “winner row.”

Patch: In the spotlight writer, relax filters to accept the best row per canonical (max score) regardless of row‑type, and default to Combined if Mid/Eve are empty. Write a short test asserting that a known winner produces ≥1 spotlight row across sections.

Join key uses Canonical where the source uses another label

Why it happens: If the writer emitted canonical_form but the enricher expects Canonical (or vice‑versa), the join fails silently.

Patch: Confirm column names in scores.csv and spotlight raw (Canonical vs canonical_form). Align them or add a column alias before the join.

Minimal validation: After applying (1) and (2), your winners evidence table should show row_score, row_type, and row_why populated for each winner in the four‑state sample you ran. The Phase‑2 “Notable finding” should vanish.

B) Canonical helper reuse (SSOT)

Replace the local _canonical in the enricher with the Stable module helper; this prevents stray edge cases (e.g., 011 vs 11, trimming, ordering) from breaking joins. It’s a one‑file change and exactly what FIX_126 flagged.
