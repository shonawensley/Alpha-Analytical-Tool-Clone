What is not automatically confirmed (and is the crucial nuance)?

A mismatch in that minimal snapshot does not automatically prove your sharepacks are wrong, because the minimal snapshot’s “freshness check” is comparing LIVE folders that can drift:

So the “red flag” is best understood as:

Red flag about live drift / environment drift, i.e., “your live tables and live draws aren’t guaranteed to be the same workbook-world at the moment you ran the snapshot.” (user)

Not, by itself, proof that the frozen sharepack snapshot is poisoned. (user)

When would it mean something is truly “wrong”?

It becomes “actually wrong” (not just a warning) if you intended the live folders to represent the staged workbook-world at that moment and they still disagree.

In other words:

If your operating rule is “after upload, the live folders are the canonical world,” then mismatches everywhere mean your canonical world is incoherent right now.

If your operating rule is “sharepacks are canonical, live folders can drift,” then mismatches are expected noise—and that’s exactly why you freeze sharepacks.

Given your stated mission (“freeze the world snapshot so it can’t drift”), the mismatches are primarily telling you:
