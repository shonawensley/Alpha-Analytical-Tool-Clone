## 4 Hit Criteria (EB / ES / VB / VS)

Across all tools we track 4 hit criteria per winner. These apply especially well to Hot Zones `TopCandidateRow`, but can be reused for Stable/V-TRAC/Digit outputs later.

Assume the winning number for the date is `W` (e.g. `694`):

- Let `digits_w = sorted(W)` (e.g. `"469"`).
- Let `vt_w` be the VT family of the winner (e.g. `"234"` or `"v234"`, depending on format).

For any **candidate triad row** in Hot Zones (from `_top_lanes`), we treat:

- `triad` = sorted 3 digits of the candidate (Hot Zones field `triad`).
- `vt_triad` = VT triad string (Hot Zones field `vt_triad`).
- `has_straight` = whether a straight lane of those digits appears in any R2/R4/R6/R8 cell (from per-item rows).
- `has_vt_straight` = whether a VT straight lane appears (per-item rows).

Then define:

1. **Exact Boxed (EB)**  
   Candidate has the exact same digits as the winner in any order.
   - `EB = (triad == digits_w)`

2. **Exact Straight (ES)**  
   Candidate is EB **and** we actually see the straight lane in the data.
   - `ES = EB AND (has_straight == 1 for at least one per-item row of this triad)`

3. **VT Boxed (VB)**  
   Candidate belongs to the same VT-TRAC family as the winner (ignoring digit identity).
   - `VB = (vt_triad == vt_w)`

4. **VT Straight (VS)**  
   Candidate is VB **and** we see a VT straight lane.
   - `VS = VB AND (has_vt_straight == 1 for at least one per-item row of this triad)`

> **Important:** all 4 hit types are useful signals. EB/ES are “exact digit” hits; VB/VS are “family” hits. During validation we don’t only care about ES – we also care whether the engine at least captured the family (VB/VS) and where those ranked.
