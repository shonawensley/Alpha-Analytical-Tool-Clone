Shape drop quality: len 2–3 best; ≥4 down‑weight unless density is extreme.

Early multi‑column Drop‑VTRAC bonus fires only when earliest_step ≤ 3 and cols_hit ≥ 2.

Clamp density/dup contributions by caps.*; then apply tanh_scale on the subtotal to avoid runaway.
