0) What analysis system did we choose?
Chosen stack: Digit Reduction → Analyzer V2 → Winners Overlay → Training Bundle with a four‑class detection taxonomy (Exact, VTRAC, Drop‑one, 3‑Value Family), earliest‑step tracking, and join‑friendly flags that tie overlay evidence back into Analyzer V2 scoring. This makes Exact and VTRAC first‑class and explicitly captures “split‑by‑one‑value” and extended 3‑value family wins.  
Why this stack:


Gives the analyzer the precise signals you care about (earliness, persistence 7→6→5, cross‑variant/set/method agreement, density/duplication) and packages them predictably for training and tuning. 


Aligns with the design charter goal: unstoppable pattern recognition, no skipped boxes, recency‑aware reasoning, and a one‑click training bundle for fast iteration. 



1) Detection & Flags (P0 – mandatory)
What to build/keep on:


Scan every numeric run in every box—no skipped boxes. 


Detection classes (per cell/run)


exact: any 3‑digit window equals a winner permutation (all 6).


vtrac: any 3‑digit window is in the winner’s local VTRAC family.


drop_exact / drop_vtrac: an exact/VTRAC window emerges after removing one contiguous run (e.g., delete “00” in …99200…).


family_exact / family_vtrac: the residual set equals the winner’s 3‑value set (or VTRAC set), even when length >3 (e.g., 599000 → family of 590).  




Carry earliest step & final match for each class: earliest_*_step, final_*_match; keep removed_run metadata for drop cases (e.g., drop_run_len, drop_digit).  


Definition of Done:


Exact, VTRAC, Drop‑one, 3‑Value family all emitted per cell with earliest_*_step, final_*_match, drop_run_len, drop_digit. 


Drop‑one removal explicitly operates on one contiguous value run, not per‑digit deletion. 


Every box has at least one of {exact, vtrac, drop_*, family_*} or none. 



2) Winner Overlay + Flags join (P0 – mandatory)
What to build/keep on:


Overlay artifacts (per state/stamp/variant):


winner_map.json (row‑level hit types, earliest steps, keys),


winner_flags.csv (join‑friendly flags for Analyzer rows),


winner_stamp.json (summary, recency labels, recent draws). 




Join keys: area, section, set, draw, col, method, mode must align so overlay flags join onto Analyzer *_per_item.csv. 


Winner family metadata in the stamp: the six permutations and the canonical VTRAC family to power near‑miss checks. 


Definition of Done:


Join succeeds with no orphaned rows; dr.win_* flags line up with overlay highlight types. 


Stamp includes {permutations[], vtrac_family[], recent_draws} and labels Set1=current, Set2=previous, Set3=two‑prior for Midday/Evening/Combined. 



3) Analyzer V2 features & scoring (P0 – mandatory)
What to build/keep on (features):


Emit earliest_step per class (exact/vtrac/drop/family).


box_family_density (how many digits inside the 3‑value family) and a dup_bonus, both capped to prevent runaway scores.


cols_hit (presence across columns 7/6/5) and variants_hit (M/E/C).


recency‑aware emergence: a Set2‑first sequence with a Set2→Set1 carryover flag.


Penalize late‑only emergence unless density is very high.


Prefer exact when present; otherwise score the nearest strong VTRAC family. 


Scoring guidance to encode:


Reward: early appearance (smaller earliest_*_step), repeated presence across columns, agreement across sets/variants/methods, and 3‑value depth (density/dups). 


Penalize: late‑only emergence or high clutter (residual uniques > 3). 


Concrete tweak example: +0.2 weight to drop_vtrac when it appears by step ≤ 3 in ≥ 2 columns. 


Definition of Done:


*_per_item.csv includes new feature columns (earliest_*_step, density/dup caps, cols_hit, variants_hit, recency flags).


*_top_candidates.csv ranks families consistent with the overlay evidence you circled in the grids (i.e., early exact/VTRAC and short “one‑run drop” cases float to the top).
