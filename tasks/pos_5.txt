What Codex added (mapped to the plan)
1) Candidate generation (✅)

Cartesian seeds from a union pool per position (not Combined‑only). This is the fix that makes 9‑8‑9 constructible when 9 is top‑K at P1 & P3 in Evening and 8 is live at P2 (E/C).

Repeat‑endcap seeds: when the same digit is top‑K at P1 and P3, build d _ d and expand P2 using both top‑K and lane bridges for the lanes where that digit is strongest. That’s the precise path that yields 989 in your Delaware example.

Lane‑concordance seeds: form a few lane‑pure combos (same lane across positions) and give them a small bonus.

✅ This directly addresses why 989 was missed: the old cartesian never formed “repeat‑endcap” or “lane‑pure” branches; the new code does.

2) Scoring with explainability (✅)

Score now sums per‑position aggregated scores and adds explicit weights for:

Cross‑variant hits (how many of C/M/E a digit occupies at that position),

Mirror‑Echo and Double‑Pressure tags,

Repeat‑endcap bonus and Lane‑concordance bonus,

Small V‑TRAC boosts (index hot / double‑family hot),

Optional Root nudge.

Each candidate carries evidence strings (e.g., P1:9 [E#1] | lanes E, Repeat endcap, V‑TRAC idx 21 hot), so users see why it ranked.

✅ This makes the shortlist finally “signal‑aware” instead of just looking like a Combined cartesian.

3) Configuration (✅)

A ShortlistConfig mapping (caps, weights, feature toggles) is loaded/merged from config (POS_SHORTLIST_CONFIG). Weights like rank, xvar, mirror_echo, double_pressure, repeat_endcap, lane_concordance, vtrac_index, vtrac_family, and optional root are all surfaced.

✅ This gives you safe knobs to tune without editing code.

4) V‑TRAC integration (⚠️ verify source)

The scorer expects two boolean‑style signals:

index heat (e.g., in Top‑10 overdue or Repeat Watch),

family hot (combo’s double family is in Top‑5).

Both are used as small additive weights, consistent with “Aux enhances, not dominates.”

⚠️ Ask Codex to confirm these are sourced from the same overlay + family rankings shown in Control Center (not recomputed). If they aren’t, have it read those cached structures instead.
