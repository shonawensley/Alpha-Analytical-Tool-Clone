Q2. “How do the triggers produce the combos I’m seeing?” (plain English)

Start with a “remaining pairs ~27–29” set (cross off pairs from recent draws until ~28 remain), then combine those pairs into boxed singles (three distinct digits). That’s the foundation list.

Now filter/score those by the active triggers:

Mirror on? keep combos that contain any mirror pair (e.g., 1&6, 2&7 …).

Root due? keep combos whose root sum is any of the due roots.

Pattern due? keep combos whose S/T pattern is in the due bucket (e.g., SST/STS/TSS group or SSS/TTT).

Floating? keep combos that include ≥ 1 floating digit.

Score by “how many active filters did this combo match,” sort, and take the top 12. That list is what you’re seeing.

The module computes five simple, repeatable signals:
