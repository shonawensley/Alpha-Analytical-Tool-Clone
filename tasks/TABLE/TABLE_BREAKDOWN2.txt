7) How Part 7 feeds the Aggregator (what values to join on)

Digit Reduction: join by lane_key / DR location, carry hz_score, hz_hot_density, hz_variants_seen to answer “is this DR box inside a genuine hot zone?”.

Stable Pattern: join by canonical/mirror‑triads; add hz_support_count, hz_max_score, hz_best_variant to distinguish “old/over‑extended” from “about to strike”.

V‑TRAC: join by VT index; use hz_index_score, hz_index_variants_seen, hz_index_recency_best_col to up‑weight “live” indexes even when the base scorer is conservative.

The winners lens (YYYYMMDD_hot_zones_winner_map.{json,csv}) sits beside the brain‑bundle and provides the minimal per‑date evidence the Aggregator needs (lanes hit, max hz score, best recency col, variant coverage).
