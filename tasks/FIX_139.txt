Why the NaNs happen (and why Fix 138 works)

Root cause: we were joining winner evidence on a string canonical that doesn’t always match. Winners use a 3‑digit canonical like 758 → 578, but the spotlight writer often emits extended substrings (0788, 007788, …). Those will never match a 3‑digit canonical join, hence row_score/row_type/row_why = NaN.

Correct join key: the Stable extractor reasons in V‑TRAC families. If we anchor everything to family_id derived from the same canonical helper (stable.canon + digits_only), extended strings and leading zeros can’t break the join.

Fix 138 does exactly that: reuse the SSOT canonical helper everywhere and join by family_id, not by raw string.
