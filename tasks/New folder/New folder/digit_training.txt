These are concrete, analyzer‑friendly features/thresholds drawn from the grids you posted.

Exact (any 3‑window equals a winner permutation).

V‑TRAC (any 3‑window is in the winner’s local mirror family).

B) “Family mass” & box stability

family_mass(run) = count of digits in the run that belong to the candidate’s 3‑value set (Exact or V‑TRAC flavor).

C) Recency‑aware emergence

D) Corridor repetition across columns (7→6→5)

cols_hit = number of columns among 7/6/5 where we see the same canonical triple (or same V‑TRAC).

Strong weight at cols_hit ≥ 2; very strong at 3/3.

E) Cross‑variant agreement (M/E/C)

variants_hit = count of variants where (exact ∪ V‑TRAC ∪ drop) appears by step ≤3.

Strong bonus at variants_hit ≥ 2.

F) Duplicates inside the family

dup_bonus = capped bonus for extra copies inside the family (e.g., 61188 > 618; 599000 > 590).

The duplicates you circled in many grids are exactly the “pressure” we want to price in.

G) One‑run‑drop quality

Record drop_run_len and drop_digit; weight 2–3‑length runs highest (typical blockers were 00/22/77).

De‑emphasize long drop runs (>4) unless the box density is extreme.

Weight hierarchy that matched what we see: Exact > V‑TRAC > Drop > 3‑Value family, modulated by earliness, persistence, box density, and cross‑variant agreement.
